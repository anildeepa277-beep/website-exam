ABOUT THE PROJECT

Bharathapuzha River Basin

Bharathapuzha, popularly known as Nila, is one of the major river systems of Kerala. This geographical information portal presents spatial information about the river basin through thematic mapping and resource assessment. The platform integrates physical geography, geological characteristics, infrastructure and water resources into an interactive mapping environment.
